# Context brief — Hearthway

Hearthway Rehoming Network is a federated network of rehoming centres and
partner shelters. Each centre takes in abandoned and surrendered dogs and
cats, screens people who want to adopt them, hands animals over, and follows
up for months afterwards. When a placement fails, the animal comes back.

## Where the network is going

Centres and teams have always run themselves. Each chooses its own forms,
spreadsheets and messaging groups, and that autonomy is why a centre can
launch an adoption campaign in a week. The network now wants every team, in
every centre, to contribute to one shared record of the people it works with
(adopters, households, fosters), so that what one team learns travels to the
next team and the next year. Teams keep their tools. A central layer pulls in
what they produce and works out which records belong to the same person.

What that would make possible, and the network cannot do today:

- Follow one adopter or household across applications, adoptions, returns
  and re-adoptions, across years and centres.
- See which screening signals (call, home visit, housing, other animals)
  predict a placement that lasts, and which predict a return.
- Answer a council funder asking "how many of last year's adoptions are
  still in place?" without weeks of reconciliation by hand.

## Capacity

You work with limited capacity to build automations and write code within
your team, so solutions need to be prioritised carefully. The expectation is
a working, operational prototype of a single source of truth two quarters
from now.

## The teams

Hearthway runs as a multi-team setup. This working example covers two teams
from the Bristol centre. Both report to the same centre coordinator, Vanessa.

**Adoptions** screens adoption requests. Most arrive during two campaigns a
year. Three staff — Megan, Graham and Jas — review every request from the
spring adoption day in March and April, ring each applicant, visit
shortlisted homes, and decide who is approved. Form responses are pasted into
a working spreadsheet that has been copied and adapted over several
campaigns.

**Follow-up** takes over once an applicant is approved. It manages the
handover of each animal, the two-week trial, follow-up calls and visits at
one and six months, and returns. Staff update their list every week.

Both teams were asked the same thing: *send us what you have on the spring
2026 adoptions.* The attached files are exactly what came back, along with
the application form behind the first one.
